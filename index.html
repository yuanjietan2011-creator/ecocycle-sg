Recycle Right Knowledge Hub

An in-depth breakdown of Singapore's strict recycling standards to prevent bin contamination.

The Blue Commingled Bin

Singapore operates a commingled single-stream recycling system. Preparation is critical to avoid ruining batches.

  • Paper & Cardboard: Fold flat boxes down. No food grease.
  • Plastics: Bottles and liquid trays must be empty and clean.

The Contamination Crisis

Around 40% of items put inside blue recycling bins currently cannot be processed because they are dirty or non-recyclable.

  • Food and Liquids: Liquid residue ruins porous paper sheets.
  • Tissue & Wipes: Used tissue papers cannot be repurposed.
