The Blue Commingled Bin

Singapore routes glass, plastics, paper, and metal fragments into one single stream. Careless mixing contaminates pure materials instantly.

  • Flattened Paper: Clear of food oils, pizza stains, or liquid tracking sheets.
  • Purified Plastics: Liquid tubs, cosmetic pumps, and milk canisters.

Contamination Red Flags

Nearly 40% of materials currently thrown into Blue Bins are incinerated immediately due to contact with wet residue.

  • Organic Matter: Leftover beverages or half-eaten snack packs.
  • Hygiene Fiber: Discarded facial tissues and sticky cleanup napkins.
