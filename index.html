Recycle Right Knowledge Hub

An in-depth breakdown of Singapore's strict recycling standards to prevent bin contamination.

The Blue Commingled Bin

Singapore operates a commingled single-stream recycling system. All valid materials go into one bin without separation, which makes preparation critical to avoid ruining batches.

  • Paper & Cardboard: Fold flat boxes down. Do not include food boxes soaked in grease.
  • Plastics: Bottles, detergent packaging, and shampoo tubs must be completely clean.
  • Metals: Clean food cans, beverage aluminum cans, and metal biscuit tins are accepted.
  • Glass: Bottles, cosmetic jars, and medicine vials. No mirrors or windows.

The Contamination Crisis

Around 40% of items put inside blue recycling bins currently cannot be processed because they are dirty, toxic, or non-recyclable.

  • Food and Liquids: Half-empty cups or leftover food ruins clean paper items around it.
  • Tissue & Wipes: Used tissues lose paper fiber integrity and are heavily contaminated with hygiene waste.
  • Styrofoam Packaging: Food containers or item packing structural blocks are strictly rejected from blue streams.

Specialized Recovery Streams

Certain classes of materials must never enter the blue bin infrastructure because they require specific sorting procedures or toxic chemical processing pipelines.

  • Beverage Containers: Return plastic drink bottles and cans to dedicated Reverse Vending Machines (RVMs) for a deposit refund.
  • Regulated E-Waste: Batteries, computers, electrical cables, charging brick adapters, and large household appliances require dedicated electronic waste collection boxes.
